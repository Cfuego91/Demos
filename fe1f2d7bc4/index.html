About us

A family company, two generations deep

Since 2000, contractors, residents and businesses have relied on MAV Plumbing for all of their plumbing needs. We are a family-owned plumbing company located in Apple Valley, California.

Our founder, Jose Martin Diaz, began working as a plumber at 17 and later decided to start his own company. His older son, Martin Diaz, took over as President in 2013. The company continues to grow, and Martin Diaz will continue to move it forward.
