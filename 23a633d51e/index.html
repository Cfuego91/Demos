How we work

A clear process, with no surprises

Every project follows the same five steps. What changes is how deep each step goes.

  1. 1

    Discover

    A conversation about your business, customers and where time is lost. If it is not a good fit, you are told.

  2. 2

    Design

    A written scope with deliverables, timeline and price, plus the structure and look of what we will build.

  3. 3

    Build

    We build in small steps and share working previews, so you can react early rather than at the end.

  4. 4

    Launch

    Checked on phone and desktop and with real examples, then released with documentation and a walkthrough.

  5. 5

    Improve

    We review how it performs with you and agree what to refine, fix or add next.

Our principles

Practical, honest and built to be maintained

  • We recommend the simplest solution that solves the problem, and say so when no AI is needed.
  • We do not promise specific results, rankings or savings. We agree measurable goals and track them.
  • AI agents hand over to a person whenever they are unsure, and their limits are documented.
  • You own what we build and receive documentation, so you are not locked in.
  • We ask only for the data a project needs and explain how it is handled.

FAQ

Questions we are often asked

How long does a project take?+

It depends on scope. You get a realistic timeline in the written proposal, based on what you need and how quickly content and feedback reach us. Automation and custom systems start with a short discovery phase before any timeline is set.

How do you price your work?+

Well-defined work such as a website is priced at a fixed amount. AI and automation projects are assessed first and then quoted. See the pricing page for details.

Who owns my website?+

Our default approach is that you own the content you provide and the finished website we build for you. The details are written into the project agreement.

Can you connect the tools I already use?+

Often yes, if they offer an API or standard integration. We check what is possible during the assessment and tell you honestly when something is not.

How is security handled?+

We use official APIs, store credentials as environment secrets, give each connection the minimum access it needs and validate all input. No system is risk-free, and we are open about the risks that remain.
