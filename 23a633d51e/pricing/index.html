Pricing

Pricing you can understand before you commit

Every project is quoted in writing before work starts. Prices are in SEK excluding VAT unless stated.

  • Website Launch

    Businesses that need a clear, credible website or landing page.

    Request a quote

    Agreed as a fixed price once the page list and content are defined.

    Includes

    • Page structure and content guidance
    • Custom, responsive design
    • Contact form delivering to your inbox
    • Basic search-engine setup
    • Deployment and handover documentation

    Not included

    • Unlimited revisions (a revision round is agreed in the proposal)
    • Ongoing hosting and maintenance unless agreed separately

    Request a quote

    Request a quote

How pricing works

  • Fixed scope where the work is well defined

    For websites and clearly bounded projects, you get a fixed price before work starts.

  • Individual assessment where it is not

    AI and automation projects depend on your tools and data, so we scope them first, then quote. You can decide to stop after the assessment.

  • Third-party costs are separate

    Domains, hosting, software subscriptions and AI usage fees are paid to the provider and shown separately, so you can see what you pay for.

  • Prices exclude VAT

    Quotes are given in SEK and state clearly whether VAT is included.
