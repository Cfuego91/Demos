AI agents for customer service and lead handling

AI agents that answer routine enquiries and qualify leads, so your team handles the conversations that matter

An AI agent is software that can read a customer's message, understand what is being asked and respond or route it, using information you have approved. We build agents for two common jobs: customer support and lead qualification.

Discuss this serviceHow pricing works

The problem

Customers expect fast replies. When the same questions arrive repeatedly, or enquiries come in while you are on a job, response times slip and leads go cold.

Our approach

An agent connected to your website or inbox that answers from a knowledge base you control, asks the right follow-up questions, collects the details you need, and hands over to a person when it is unsure or when the customer asks.

Who it is for

Businesses that receive repeated questions by email, web chat or contact forms, and teams that want enquiries sorted and summarised before a person picks them up.

What is included

  • Agreeing which questions the agent may answer and which it must hand over
  • A knowledge base built from your own, approved information
  • Lead qualification questions tailored to your services
  • Hand-over to a person by email or into your existing tools
  • Testing with realistic enquiries before it talks to real customers
  • Clear wording that tells customers they are talking to an automated assistant

Typical use cases

  • Answering opening hours, service area and process questions
  • Collecting job details and photos from a prospective customer
  • Summarising an enquiry and notifying the right person
  • After-hours first response with a promise to follow up

How the work runs

  1. 1

    Scope the conversations

    We map the questions and enquiries you receive and decide what the agent should and should not do.

  2. 2

    Prepare the knowledge

    We collect and structure approved answers so the agent has reliable source material.

  3. 3

    Build and test

    We build the agent and test it with real examples, including difficult and off-topic messages.

  4. 4

    Launch and review

    We launch in a controlled way and review real conversations together to improve the answers.

Expected outcome: Faster first responses and less repetitive typing for your team. The intended benefit is that fewer enquiries go unanswered and your people spend time on qualified conversations. AI agents can make mistakes, so we design for supervision and easy hand-over rather than full autonomy.

Questions about ai agents for customer service and lead handling

Can the AI give a wrong answer?+

Yes, any language model can. That is why we restrict the agent to approved information, add hand-over rules, and test it before launch. We also recommend reviewing conversations regularly.

Will customers know they are talking to an AI?+

We recommend telling them clearly. Transparency builds trust, and in some situations it is a legal expectation.

Where does customer data go?+

That depends on the tools chosen. We explain which providers process the data before we build, and we avoid collecting more than is needed. See our FAQ on data handling.

Tell us what you need help with

Describe your situation and what you would like to improve. We will reply with honest next steps, including when we think something is not worth building.
