Services

Websites, AI agents and automation that fit how you work

Pick one service or combine them. Each page explains the problem it solves, what is included and what to expect.

  • Business websites

    Fast, clear websites and landing pages built to turn visitors into enquiries.

    About business websites

    Typically includes

    • Page structure and copy guidance written around what your customers search for
    • Responsive design for phones, tablets and desktops
    • Contact form with spam protection, delivering enquiries to your inbox
    • Search-friendly structure: page titles, descriptions, sitemap and clean URLs

  • AI agents for customer service and lead handling

    AI assistants that answer common questions and qualify enquiries, with a human in the loop.

    About ai agents for customer service and lead handling

    Typically includes

    • Agreeing which questions the agent may answer and which it must hand over
    • A knowledge base built from your own, approved information
    • Lead qualification questions tailored to your services
    • Hand-over to a person by email or into your existing tools

  • Business process automation

    Remove repetitive admin by automating predictable steps between your tools.

    About business process automation

    Typically includes

    • A workflow review that documents how the work happens today
    • A clear list of what to automate, what to leave manual and why
    • Implementation with the tools you already use where possible
    • Error handling and notifications so failures are visible, not silent

  • Custom AI systems and internal tools

    Tailor-made tools that use AI where it helps and ordinary software where it does not.

    About custom ai systems and internal tools

    Typically includes

    • A written scope that states the problem, users and what success looks like
    • A working first version that your team can test early
    • AI components with clear limits, review steps and fallbacks
    • Access control and sensible handling of business data

  • API integrations

    Connect the applications you already use so data moves without retyping.

    About api integrations

    Typically includes

    • A map of your tools, data and how they should connect
    • Integration with the applications' official APIs where available
    • Careful handling of credentials, with each integration given only the access it needs
    • Logging and alerts so failures are visible
