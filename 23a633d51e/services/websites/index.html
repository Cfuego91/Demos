Business websites

A business website that explains what you do and makes it easy to get in touch

Many small businesses have a website that is out of date, hard to use on a phone, or does not say clearly what they sell. We design and build sites that fix that: clear messaging, quick load times and an obvious next step for the visitor.

Discuss this serviceHow pricing works

The problem

Potential customers check your website before they call. If it looks dated, loads slowly or buries your contact details, they often move on to the next company in the search results.

Our approach

A custom-designed, mobile-first website with your own content, your brand colours and a clear structure: what you do, who you do it for, proof that you can be trusted, and a simple way to contact you.

Who it is for

Contractors, tradespeople, professional services and local businesses that want a credible online presence, and companies that are launching a new offer and need a dedicated landing page.

What is included

  • Page structure and copy guidance written around what your customers search for
  • Responsive design for phones, tablets and desktops
  • Contact form with spam protection, delivering enquiries to your inbox
  • Search-friendly structure: page titles, descriptions, sitemap and clean URLs
  • Accessibility basics: keyboard navigation, readable contrast, labelled forms
  • Deployment to hosting you own or control, with setup documentation

Typical use cases

  • Replacing an outdated company website
  • A focused landing page for one service or campaign
  • Adding service pages and clear calls to action to an existing brand
  • Preparing a site that can later connect to booking, CRM or AI tools

How the work runs

  1. 1

    Discovery

    We learn what you sell, who your customers are and which pages and actions matter most.

  2. 2

    Structure and content

    We agree the page structure and write or refine the content together with you.

  3. 3

    Design and build

    We design and build the site, sharing a working preview you can review on your own phone.

  4. 4

    Launch and handover

    We connect your domain, test the contact form and hand over documentation.

Expected outcome: A site that looks credible, loads quickly and makes contacting you effortless. The business benefit is more of the right enquiries and fewer lost to a weak first impression. We cannot promise a specific number of leads, because that depends on your market and traffic.

Questions about business websites

Can you use my existing text and photos?+

Yes. We can reuse your existing content, improve it, or write new copy with you. Real photos of your work usually perform better than stock images.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?+

That depends on the setup we agree. We can build a simple content structure you can edit, or handle updates for you. We will confirm which approach fits before we start.

Do you also handle hosting and the domain?+

We can set up hosting and connect your domain. Our default approach is that the domain and accounts are registered in your name, so you stay in control.

Tell us what you need help with

Describe your situation and what you would like to improve. We will reply with honest next steps, including when we think something is not worth building.
