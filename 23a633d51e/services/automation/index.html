Business process automation

Automate the repetitive admin that eats into your working week

Most businesses have tasks that follow the same steps every time: copying details between systems, sending reminders, preparing documents, chasing replies. We find those tasks and automate the ones that are safe and worthwhile to automate.

Discuss this serviceHow pricing works

The problem

Manual, repetitive work is slow and easy to get wrong. It also keeps skilled people busy with administration instead of paid or customer-facing work.

Our approach

We analyse the workflow first, then automate the steps that are rule-based and repeatable, keeping a person in control of decisions that need judgement.

Who it is for

Owner-managed businesses and small teams with predictable routines such as quotes, bookings, invoicing follow-up, onboarding or lead follow-up.

What is included

  • A workflow review that documents how the work happens today
  • A clear list of what to automate, what to leave manual and why
  • Implementation with the tools you already use where possible
  • Error handling and notifications so failures are visible, not silent
  • Documentation so you understand what runs and how to change it
  • A review period after launch to adjust what we got wrong

Typical use cases

  • Sending a lead's details to your CRM and notifying the right person
  • Automatic reminders and follow-up for quotes and bookings
  • Preparing standard documents from form submissions
  • Weekly summaries gathered from several systems

How the work runs

  1. 1

    Workflow analysis

    We follow the work from start to finish and measure where time and mistakes accumulate.

  2. 2

    Prioritise

    We choose the few automations with the best value and lowest risk to start with.

  3. 3

    Implement

    We build and test the automation with real examples, including edge cases.

  4. 4

    Monitor and improve

    We watch the first runs, fix surprises and document the final setup.

Expected outcome: Less manual work and fewer copy-paste errors in the processes we automate. The size of the benefit depends on how often the task occurs, so we estimate it together before building rather than promising savings in advance.

Questions about business process automation

Do I need to change the software I use?+

Usually not. We start with the tools you already have and connect them. We will tell you honestly if a tool cannot be connected reliably.

What happens if an automation fails?+

We build in failure alerts and logging so problems are noticed and fixed, and we document how to pause or switch off an automation.

Which tasks should not be automated?+

Anything that needs judgement, carries legal risk, or happens too rarely to repay the effort. We will say so rather than automate for the sake of it.

Tell us what you need help with

Describe your situation and what you would like to improve. We will reply with honest next steps, including when we think something is not worth building.
