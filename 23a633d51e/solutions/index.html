How this looks in your kind of business

These are illustrative examples of the problems we solve. They describe typical situations, not past clients or guaranteed results.

Contractors and tradespeople

You are on a roof, under a sink or on a building site when the phone rings. Enquiries and quote requests pile up until evening.

Common pain points

  • Missed enquiries while working
  • Quote requests with missing details
  • A website that does not explain your services

What we could build

  • Structured quote requests

    A form or assistant that collects job type, location and photos, so you can price faster.

  • A clear service website

    Pages for each service and area you cover, with an obvious way to get in touch.

  • Follow-up reminders

    Automatic nudges for quotes that have not been answered.

Professional service businesses

Consultants, accountants and advisers spend billable time on enquiries, scheduling and routine questions.

Common pain points

  • Repeated questions about process and fees
  • Manual onboarding steps
  • Unclear who should reply to which enquiry

What we could build

  • Enquiry triage

    Incoming messages summarised and routed to the right person.

  • Onboarding automation

    Standard welcome emails and document requests sent when a new client is accepted.

  • Internal knowledge search

    Staff can ask questions about internal procedures in plain language.

Local service providers

Salons, clinics, cleaners and similar businesses rely on bookings and word of mouth, and lose customers to slow replies.

Common pain points

  • Questions about hours and prices by phone and message
  • No-shows and last-minute changes
  • An outdated or missing website

What we could build

  • A fast, local website

    Opening hours, services, prices where appropriate and a clear contact route.

  • Automated first response

    An immediate reply that confirms the message was received and asks for key details.

  • Booking and reminders

    Connecting your booking tool to reminders and notifications.

E-commerce businesses

Online shops answer the same questions about orders, returns and delivery every day, and move data between several systems.

Common pain points

  • Repetitive customer service questions
  • Orders and stock handled across separate tools
  • Manual reporting

What we could build

  • Order-status answers

    An assistant that answers common delivery and returns questions from approved information.

  • System connections

    Orders, inventory and accounting kept in sync through APIs.

  • Weekly summary

    A single report assembled automatically from your shop and ad accounts.

Businesses with repetitive administration

Any business where the same information is typed several times, documents are prepared by hand, or follow-ups depend on memory.

Common pain points
